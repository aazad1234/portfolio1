A little context

I care about the parts users feel.

A product can look finished and still feel difficult. I work on the moments that decide whether it feels effortless: the first load, the empty state, the keyboard path, the error message that tells you what to do next.

That usually means moving between design and engineering rather than treating them as separate phases. It also means keeping the implementation simple enough for the team to keep improving after launch.

How I work

From ambiguity to a calm release.

A simple cadence with visible decisions, reviewable slices and no mystery about what happens next.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Clarify the goal, the constraints and the people the product is for. We agree on what success looks like before building.

  2. 02

    Define

    Map the core flows, content and system boundaries. I turn the brief into a scoped plan with visible assumptions.

  3. 03

    Build

    Work in small, reviewable slices with working previews. Quality checks happen alongside the build, not after it.

  4. 04

    Launch & refine

    Ship with the right documentation and a short feedback loop. We fix what the real world reveals.
