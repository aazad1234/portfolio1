The short version

Good software feels considered.

With over five years of experience in web development, I've helped product teams build and scale digital experiences from first sketch to production. My work sits where thoughtful interface design meets dependable engineering.

I specialise in JavaScript technologies across the stack and have professional experience working with React, Node.js and modern cloud infrastructure. I care about the details that users feel: fast pages, obvious interactions, resilient systems and accessible flows.

Working principles

What I keep coming back to.

Not a manifesto — just the defaults that keep the work honest.

01

Clarity over cleverness

The simplest solution that the next developer can understand is usually the right one.

02

Fast is a feature

Performance is part of the experience. I treat speed as a product requirement, not a cleanup task.

03

Accessible by default

Keyboard access, clear focus states and honest semantics belong in the first build, not the last.

04

Ship, learn, refine

Small, measurable releases beat large speculative launches. Feedback is part of the architecture.
