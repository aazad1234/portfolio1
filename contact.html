Before you write

A few useful details.

No formal brief required. These answers cover the most common starting questions.

What kinds of projects are a good fit?

Product teams that need a full-stack builder who cares about design quality, accessibility and maintainability. Web applications, internal tools, marketing sites with custom interactions and focused product experiments are all a good fit.

Do you work with existing teams?

Yes. I can join an existing workflow, work alongside designers and engineers, and adapt to the tools and conventions the team already uses. Clear ownership and regular communication matter more than a particular process.

How do you handle timelines?

I scope work in small slices, communicate assumptions early and keep a visible sequence of deliverables. After a short discovery conversation, I can propose a realistic plan with milestones and checkpoints.

Can you work remotely?

Yes. I work remotely with teams across time zones and am based in Pacific Time. I keep communication asynchronous by default and schedule working sessions when they are useful.

What happens after launch?

You receive the implementation, setup notes and a handover. If you want ongoing support, we can agree on a follow-up window or a lighter maintenance arrangement.
