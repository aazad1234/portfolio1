Principles

The defaults behind the decisions.

01

Clarity over cleverness

The simplest solution that the next developer can understand is usually the right one.

02

Fast is a feature

Performance is part of the experience. I treat speed as a product requirement, not a cleanup task.

03

Accessible by default

Keyboard access, clear focus states and honest semantics belong in the first build, not the last.

04

Ship, learn, refine

Small, measurable releases beat large speculative launches. Feedback is part of the architecture.
