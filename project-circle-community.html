Circle — Community Platform

A full-stack community concept with real-time feeds, direct messages and moderation tools that stay out of the way.

01 — Context

The challenge

Circle explores what a smaller, more intentional social product could feel like. The concept combines a calm feed, lightweight profiles and real-time conversations with a moderation model designed for small communities rather than public virality.

Community products have to balance expression, safety and attention. Most tools optimise for engagement at all costs. This concept asked a different question: what would a community platform look like if it respected members' time and gave moderators clear, humane controls?

02 — Approach

The solution

I designed a feed with deliberate pacing, a conversation model that keeps threads readable, and a moderation queue that surfaces context before action. The backend uses an event-driven approach with WebSockets for presence and messages, plus a small set of guardrails for spam and repeated reports.

03 — Build

What I built

01

Intentional feed

Chronological by default, with category filters instead of an engagement algorithm.

02

Real-time conversations

Presence, typing states and message delivery updates over WebSockets.

03

Moderation workspace

A queue that shows the surrounding conversation and prior reports.

04

Small-community profiles

Simple member pages focused on contribution rather than follower counts.

04 — Result

What the build demonstrates

  • A feed designed for slower, more intentional participation.
  • Threaded conversations that remain readable on mobile.
  • Moderation tools with context, not just a delete button.
  • Real-time interactions with sensible offline fallbacks.

“Real-time features are easy to add and hard to make calm. The best version of Circle was the one where the interface told you only what you needed to know, when you needed to know it.”
